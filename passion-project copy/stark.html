Winter is coming

The Starks of Winterfell are a major family in the game of thrones universe. They are the wardens of the north meaning all other houses in the north are their bannermen. Their house slogan is "winter is coming" signifying their connection to the north. Their sigil is a dierwolf which is a type of myhtical wolf from north of the wall. They are much larger than wolves and much more dangerous.
