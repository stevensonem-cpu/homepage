Hello World!

Reading and writing,        despite my frustration     , has led me to make connections with the most important and influential people in my life. Deborah Brandt’s concept of “literacy sponsors” allows me to reiterate who these people are. They are my sponsors and the people I have sponsored.


We have connected through literacy and experience which was imperative to how I write and read in my daily life. Writing and I are not on the best terms, but I will continue to hold it in high regard for what I have gained from it.
